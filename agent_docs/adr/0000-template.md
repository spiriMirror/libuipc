# ADR 0000 — Decision Title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: subsystem or team
- Implements: commit/PR when known
- Supersedes: ADR number or N/A

## Context

Describe the concrete pressure, constraints, and failure modes. Link the source
and tests that establish the facts.

## Decision

State the chosen rule precisely enough that a future change can be reviewed
against it.

## Consequences

List intended benefits, accepted costs, compatibility effects, and follow-up
work. Record what is explicitly *not* guaranteed.

## Alternatives considered

Summarize credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Validation

Record the tests, builds, benchmarks, sanitizers, or rollout evidence that made
the decision acceptable.
